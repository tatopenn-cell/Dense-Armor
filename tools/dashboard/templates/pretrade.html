Check my orders

Every order of your file goes to the pre-trade checker, one after the other in time order. The controls are the five of SEC Rule 15c3-5 (c)(1): size, price band, duplicate, rate and notional; the first one that fails is the reason. The file needs a header with the columns account (or stock, symbol, ticker), side (buy or sell), size (or shares, quantity, qty) and price; ref_price and a time (seconds or an ISO date) are optional. Without a time the orders are one second apart.
